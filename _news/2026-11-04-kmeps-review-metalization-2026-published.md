- Title: Review of Metallization Technologies for Next-Generation Semiconductor Packaging Interconnects ([link](https://journal.kmeps.or.kr/html/?pmode=archivedetail&seq=423))

This review examines metallization technologies for next-generation semiconductor packaging interconnects, where shrinking linewidths, fine trenches, high-aspect-ratio vias, and high-frequency conduction losses make reliable wiring increasingly difficult.

The paper first discusses the limits of conventional copper interconnects and the approaches for lowering resistance through microstructure control and thermal treatment, along with barrierless alternative metals such as Ru and Mo. It then reviews semi-additive processing and electroplating for fine-pitch and vertical interconnects, compares the filling mechanisms of through-silicon vias (TSVs) and through-glass vias (TGVs), and introduces meta-conductors as a way to reduce high-frequency conductor loss.

The review concludes that different interconnect levels may require different metallization strategies.

The work was carried out by Prof. Jimin Kwon's group at KAIST DSIL, with Hyeongjun Kim as first author, together with Woomin Jeon, Yongwoo Lee, and Haksoon Jung.

This research was supported by the Korea Electronics Technology Institute (KETI) and the Samsung Research Funding & Incubation Center of Samsung Electronics.
