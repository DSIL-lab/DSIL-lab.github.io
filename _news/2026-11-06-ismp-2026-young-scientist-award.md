Kyungsun Kim received a Young Scientist Award at ISMP 2026, hosted by the Korean Microelectronics and Packaging Society (KMEPS).

The awarded work, co-authored with Haksoon Jung, Nahyeon Kim, Yongwoo Lee, and Prof. Jimin Kwon, presented a monolithic 3D-printed antenna-in-package that integrates RF components within a single printed structure.
