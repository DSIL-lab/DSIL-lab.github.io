  - Dr. Youngmin Jo, a postdoctoral researcher, has been selected for the 2026 Basic Research Program B (Inclusive Type) under the Ministry of Education's Academic Research Support Program in Science and Engineering, administered by the National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF).  

    Selected project: 
      - Oxide Heterojunction Tunnel Field-Effect Transistors for Ultra-Low-Power Embedded Memory Systems

    
    The project applies Type-II oxide heterojunction thin films as the tunneling channel of a vertical-channel transistor, aiming to achieve a subthreshold swing below 60 mV/dec — the physical limit of conventional thermionic emission — together with low-voltage operation. The work will be validated in embedded DRAM cell devices to demonstrate power reduction.

    The program strengthens the independent research capabilities of non-tenure-track faculty and postdoctoral researchers at universities, providing three years of funding (Sept. 2026 – Aug. 2029).
