- Title: Design of a Samsung 28-nm CMOS-Based Active Silicon Interposer Bridge for High-Speed Chiplet Systems ([link](https://journal.kmeps.or.kr/html/?pmode=archivedetail&seq=437))

This work proposes an active silicon interposer bridge that uses CMOS repeaters to regenerate attenuated signals in fine-pitch redistribution layers (RDLs) for high-speed chiplet systems.

As RDL pitches shrink and data rates increase, passive channel optimization alone can no longer provide sufficient signal integrity. To address this, the authors designed CMOS repeaters in a Samsung 28-nm process and inserted them along the RDL channel within the interposer bridge. Repeater performance and power consumption depend on transistor sizing and repeater spacing.

For a 6 mm passive channel with 0.8 µm line/space at 12 Gb/s, no effective eye opening was obtained. With repeaters inserted, the eye width reached about 0.97 UI and the eye height reached 100% of VDD. By comparing signal integrity and energy per bit across repeater spacings and inverter sizes, the work derives a design guideline that minimizes the number of repeater stages while keeping operation reliable.

The work was carried out by Prof. Jimin Kwon's group at KAIST DSIL, with Yurim Choi as first author, together with Yongwoo Lee, Haksoon Jung, and Jonghyeon Lee.
